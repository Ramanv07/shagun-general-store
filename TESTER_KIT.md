# Shagun Mart — Closed Testing Playbook (Tester Kit)

Namaste! Shagun Mart app ke Closed Testing program me join hone ke liye shukriya. Aapka testing hamare liye bohot zaroori hai taaki app Play Store par public release ke liye approve ho sake.

Kripya niche diye gaye steps ko dhyan se follow karein:

---

## 1. Closed Testing Me Kaise Join Karein & Install Karein

1. **Email Invite Accept Karein:**
   - Hum aapke Gmail address par Google Play Closed Test ka invitation bhejenge.
   - Invite link par click karein aur **"Become a Tester" / "Accept Invitation"** button par tap karein.
2. **App Download Karein:**
   - Invitation accept karne ke baad **"Download on Google Play"** link par click karein.
   - Google Play Store open hoga aur aap **Shagun Mart** app ko install kar payenge.
3. **14 Days Tak App Ko Phone Me Rakhein:**
   - **BOHOT ZAROORI:** Google ke niyam anusar sabhi 20 testers ko app kam se kam **14 din lagatar** apne phone me installed rakhni hai. App ko beech me uninstall na karein.

---

## 2. Weekly Testing Schedule (Aapko Kya-Kya Try Karna Hai)

Google check karta hai ki testers app ko sach me use kar rahe hain ya nahi. Kripya hafte me 2 se 3 baar app open karein aur ye features test karein:

### Week 1 (Days 1–7): Exploration & Core Features
- [ ] **Account Login / Register:** Naya account banayein ya OTP / Google se login karein.
- [ ] **Browse Categories:** Personal Care, Skincare, Household, Toys, aur Bridal section check karein.
- [ ] **Search Bar:** Kisi product ka naam type karke search karein (jaise "rice", "cream", "shampoo").
- [ ] **Address Management:** Account section me jaakar apna delivery address add aur edit karein.
- [ ] **Place a Test Order:** Cart me item add karein aur **Cash on Delivery (COD)** select karke test order place karein. *(Test accounts ke liye real UPI band hai, isliye koi actual paise nahi katenge).*
- [ ] **Order Tracking:** Orders tab me jakar apne test order ka status dekhein.

### Week 2 (Days 8–14): Feedback & Update Verification
- [ ] **App Update Install Karein:** Jab hum naya version (e.g. versionCode bump) upload karenge, Play Store se app update karein.
- [ ] **Send Feedback Button:** Account tab me jakar **"Send Feedback"** par tap karein aur app ki speed, design ya kisi bug ke baare me apna real feedback likhein.
- [ ] **Product Reviews:** Delivered product par jakar 5-star rating ya review comment de kar dekhein. Review par **Report** button bhi test karein.
- [ ] **Wishlist & Cart Management:** Items ko cart se remove ya quantity badha/ghata kar dekhein.

---

## 3. DO's and DON'Ts (Zaroori Niyam)

| ✅ Kya Karein (DO) | ❌ Kya Na Karein (DON'T) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Haftemein 2-3 baar app zaroor open karein | 14 din se pehle app ko uninstall mat karein |
| Order place karte waqt **Cash on Delivery (COD)** hi chunein | Koi real paise transfer karne ki koshish na karein |
| Jo bhi problem ya suggestion ho, in-app "Send Feedback" se bhejein | Google Play par fake review ya irrelevant comment na karein |
| Play Store opt-in status 14 din tak active rakhein | Test link kisi unauthorized vyakti ko share na karein |
